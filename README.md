# Mandible: War Beneath the Grass

A real-time strategy game at ant scale. Queens ascend through five levels, unlocking new castes and heroes that level up to 10; wild beetles, spiders and wasps guard the richest food with their own special attacks. Choose your colony: **black garden ants** (Lasius niger: huge numbers, strong trails, honeydew farmers, formic acid) or **red fire ants** (Solenopsis invicta: venom stings, mound fury, protein-hungry predators). Workers forage on their own along real pheromone trails; each kind of food does something different (sugar speeds workers, fruit heals, insect protein makes the queen lay faster, seeds are dense but heavy). Fallen ants become food, and you win by killing the enemy queen. The in-game **Field Guide** explains every caste, bug and food, how real ants find food, and what a colony needs, with live 3D portraits.

Built with Godot 4.7 (.NET / C#). Every model, texture, sound and music track is generated by the project itself. The only external assets are the Cinzel and Inter fonts (SIL Open Font License, in `assets/fonts`).

## The nest

A match begins the way a real colony does: a queen and eight workers on bare soil. The workers dig the shaft and royal chamber (a plug of soil over the hole sinks as they go, with the progress shown above it), then the queen walks down to her chamber and only then starts to lay. Every room after that is dug by workers too: brood chambers near the warm surface set how many ants you can keep, stores at mid depth set how much food you can hold, and the upgrade rooms carry the colony's research. New ants hatch below and climb out of the shaft. Enemies must break in through the entrance to reach the queen; warriors can garrison inside. Press **N** for Glass Earth: the ground over your nest turns translucent and shows the galleries and rooms at their real depths while the surface stays in view.

## Lane War (hero mode)

Main menu, then **Lane War**. The two colonies face each other across the yard, left and right, so the lanes run along a phone held sideways. Three lanes run between the nests. Every 30 seconds each mound sends free waves of soldiers down every lane (gunners join after 5 minutes, majors after 10, elite waves after 15; waves grow a level stronger every 5 minutes). Each lane has two **guardian posts**, a rooted major with an acid gunner behind it, and four more guardians stand at each queen's entrance; the queen cannot be hurt until all of hers fall. Guardians are part of the mound: the colony repairs one that is left alone for a few seconds, and a hero attacking a post with none of its own creeps at the post's feet does only a third of its damage, so posts fall to a pushed lane, not to a lone hero slipping past the fight. You steer one **hero** (Warlord, Acid Matron or Sky Marshal): right-click to move or attack, **Q** for its ability, **1-4** to buy Mandibles, Carapace, Swift Legs or Royal Jelly with food from kills and your foragers, **Space** to find it. It levels to 10, heals in the pool at your nest and respawns after a short timer. Wild bugs live in the jungle between the lanes. Kill the enemy queen to win.

**Mound guardians in Colony War:** on the difficulty screen (and in the multiplayer lobby, host's choice) switch *Mound guardians* on and every nest entrance starts with the same four-guardian guard: two majors and two acid gunners grown into the mound, rooted, repaired by the colony, and taking no population. Raids then have to break a gate, not just walk in.

## Phones and tablets

The game follows the device's rotation: landscape either way, or portrait (the selection panel moves to the top).

## Multiplayer (LAN and internet, Windows / macOS / Android / iOS)

Main menu, then **Multiplayer**. One player hosts, the other joins. The host picks the mode (Colony War or Lane War) and a colony (black garden ants or red fire ants); the guest leads the other colony. In Lane War each player picks a hero in the lobby. Both players need the same release.

- **Same Wi-Fi or LAN:** choose *Host on this network*. The game appears automatically in the other player's *Games on your network* list.
- **Over the internet:** choose *Host over the internet*. Mandible asks the router to open UDP port 47010 (UPnP) and shows your internet address to send to your friend, who types it into *Join*. If the router refuses, forward UDP 47010 to the host PC manually.
- Phones on mobile data usually cannot *host* (carrier NAT) but can join a PC host.

How it works: **deterministic lockstep**. Both machines run the identical simulation at a fixed 30 ticks a second; only player orders cross the network (a few hundred bytes a second), so it stays light on mobile data. Orders are grouped into turns of 3 ticks and scheduled a few turns ahead (2 on a LAN, up to 6 on slow links, chosen from the measured ping), so both machines apply every order on exactly the same tick. A machine never runs ahead of the orders it has received; if a packet is late the game pauses for a moment instead of guessing. Every 10 turns the machines compare a checksum of the whole game state; a mismatch is reported in the HUD.

Tested: two-process matches with 0 desyncs; 240 ms and 500 ms round trips with jitter run at full speed with no stalls (input delay adapts to 2 and 5 turns); and `--synctest` trails are bit-identical on Windows x64 and macOS ARM64.

Touch: tap to select, drag to box-select, tap the ground to order the selection, long-press for attack-move, double-tap empty ground to deselect, two fingers to pan / pinch-zoom / twist-rotate.

Graphics: Main menu, then *Graphics* cycles High / Medium / Low. Phones and tablets start on Low.

## Layout

| Path | What |
|---|---|
| `src/Sim` | Simulation: ants, foraging, pheromones, combat, colonies, crumb showers |
| `src/World` | Procedural, mirrored backyard: soil, leaves, twigs, rocks, grass, food, nests |

| `src/UI` | HUD, minimap, theme |
| `src/Audio` | Positional audio, swarm layer, adaptive music |
| `tools/gen_audio.py` | Synthesises every sound and music track into `assets/audio` |

## Testing tools

```bash
powershell -File tools/capture.ps1 captures
```

The command above runs a scripted screenshot tour plus an input self-test (box select, orders, hotkeys).

```bash
"D:\Tools\Godot\Godot_v4.7.2-stable_mono_win64\Godot_v4.7.2-stable_mono_win64_console.exe" --headless --path . -- --simtest=1800,Normal,Hard
```

The command above plays a full AI-vs-AI match without graphics and prints economy, army and frame-time stats every minute.

```bash
godot --headless --path . -- --nettest=host,600
godot --headless --path . -- --nettest=join:127.0.0.1,600
```

The two commands above run a full networked AI-vs-AI match between two processes and report checksum matches and desyncs.

\\ash
powershell -File tools/balance.ps1 -Diff Normal -Games 8
\\n
The command above plays black vs red on 8 different maps at once and prints who won each. `tools/lanebalance.ps1` does the same for every Lane War hero matchup.

`--synctest=600` plays fixed matches in both modes and prints the state checksum every 30 s: compare the trail across platforms (the macOS and Android-emulator workflows run it in the cloud). Set `MANDIBLE_NETLAG=250` before a `--nettest` pair to simulate a slow, jittery internet link.
