## What each target asks of the game

- **A**: thick glass with green edges and a jagged fracture, loose shards that catch the light, a cotton plug, a water reservoir behind a second plug, and a handwritten label. The tube sits half sunk in leaf litter. The comic panels must show this same tube.
- **B**: stones from at least five rock species and many sizes, all in warm tones, sunk and tilted, clustered with bare gaps between clusters. Grass grows in uneven clumps of mixed species, with some dry blades. A trail is worn into the soil. Moss sits on the shady side of stones. No mesh is repeated across an area.
- **C**: one shaft that wanders down from the entrance crater, with chambers hanging off it at uneven depths like fruit on a stem. Chambers are never in rows or columns. Level 1 is the small chamber just under the entrance and the last level is the big royal chamber at the bottom. Soil layers run top to bottom: humus, ochre clay, golden sand, gravel. Chambers you have dug are lit and full; the rest are dark.
- **D**: claw-scraped walls with embedded pebbles and roots, brood sorted into eggs, larvae and cocoons, warm light falling from above into shadow, and a side tunnel leading off.

- **E**: each food type has to be recognisable at a glance and never look like a pebble. Crumbs have a crust edge and a porous inside. Seeds include striped sunflower seeds and grass seeds, with split, empty husks. Sugar is crystals or a sticky amber drop. Honeydew is clear droplets on a leaf near aphids. Berries are glossy and translucent, with stems, and some are bruised. Fruit pieces have browned cut faces. Insect protein is a real dead beetle or a torn section of caterpillar. Each pile varies in size and orientation, and pieces show bites as the pile runs down.
- **F**: ground never explored is pure black on the terrain, objects, ants and minimap. The edge is soft and irregular, a few ants wide, and bulges around each ant. A level opens showing only the trail and a narrow band either side. Explored ground that is out of sight stays dimmed as it is today; the target does not show that state.

Targets A, B, D and E are framed closer than the gameplay camera. The review gate compares materials, variety, light and palette at the game's own framing; it does not ask for a pixel match.
